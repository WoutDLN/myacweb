## Description

This workshop, part of the [IDE](https://www.i-d-e.de/)'s 2016 Summer School in Digital Humanities, moved beyond the very basics of TEI-XML (taught the previous day) by teaching students how to start transcribing textual documents, with a focus on handwritten materials.
